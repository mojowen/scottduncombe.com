Financial App Privacy Policy.

How the Financial app collects, uses, deletes, and safeguards your data.

Data Collection

We collect data from you when you sign up for our app and provide us with your financial history. This includes data related to your income, expenses, savings, investments, and other relevant financial information. We also collect data on your net worth over time, which we use to provide you with insights and help you make informed financial decisions.

Data Usage

We do not sell, use, or make your data visible to anyone else without your express approval. We use your data solely to provide you with personalized financial insights and help you track your net worth over time. We may also use your data to improve our app and provide you with better services.

Data Deletion

You have the right to request that we delete your data at any time. Upon receiving your request, we will delete your data from our servers and any third-party services that we use to provide you with our app.

Data Security

We take data security seriously and engage in industry best practices to keep your data safe and secure. This includes utilizing industry-standard encryption-at-rest and in-flight and utilizing retention policies to protect your data from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We also monitor our systems regularly to ensure that they are secure and up-to-date.

By using our app, you agree to the terms outlined in this privacy policy. If you have any questions or concerns about how we collect, use, or safeguard your data, please contact us at admin@scottduncombe.com.
