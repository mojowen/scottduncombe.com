Power the Polls

Recruiting the next generation of poll workers when COVID threatened to close every polling place in America.

Recognizing a severe shortage of poll workers in 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic — and the effect that would have on voting — civic and corporate leaders came together to establish a new organization to recruit the next generation of poll workers to fill the gap.

We set an ambitious goal: deploying a new recruiting platform targeting 250,000 poll workers, driven by paid advertising, influencer engagement, and nonprofit and corporate partners. Over the next four months we recruited 710,939 new poll-worker sign-ups through powerthepolls.org — thanks to several hundred partner organizations spreading the word.

Generated press coverage from The Daily Show, NPR, Vogue, NBC, ABC — and even CNN and NPR quoting the recruitment effort as it happened.

The site was built in StencilJS, with the embeddable partner form published as an NPM package so any partner could drop sign-up directly into their own site — hundreds did.
