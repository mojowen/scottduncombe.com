## Content

MoodMonitor is clinical software that keeps providers and patients in touch through
treatment for treatment-resistant depression.

I co-founded MoodMonitor in 2016 with Noah Manger. Ketamine was emerging as a
breakthrough treatment for depression, but outcomes depend on tracking patients'
mood closely between sessions — and only about 11% of patients keep up a paper mood
journal. We built MoodMonitor to close that gap: a HIPAA-compliant platform where
patients receive a daily check-in by text message, complete clinically verified
surveys every other week, and both patient and doctor watch progress chart in real
time together. By the time we joined Osmind, more than 50 ketamine clinics and their
patients nationwide used it, with over 90% of patients keeping up daily monitoring
via text message.

In September 2020, MoodMonitor merged into [Osmind](https://www.osmind.org), the
leading EHR platform for interventional psychiatry.

**The merger story (from the original blog post, archived):**

The ketamine and psychedelic therapy landscape evolved rapidly — new technologies, practices, and funders entered the space with great resources. We saw that MoodMonitor needed to change to keep providing the best possible service to our clinics and patients — and we're very excited to announce that change. MoodMonitor joined forces with Osmind.

We strongly believe Osmind is the best choice for ketamine practices and the future of ketamine and psychedelic therapy. They are already used all across the country and Lucia, Jimmy, and the Osmind team are aligned with our mission and care as deeply about patients as we do. We trust them to continue our work together helping patients.

We will no longer be working full-time on the project, but stayed on as advisors at Osmind and are available to talk at any time. Osmind continued to operate MoodMonitor for existing clinics so no patients lost care.

## Facts

- Founded: 2016, by Scott Duncombe & Noah Manger
- Product: SMS-based daily mood + pain monitoring; clinically verified surveys;
  shared charts for clinician and patient; HIPAA compliant
- Reach: 50+ ketamine clinics nationwide at merger
- Outcome: merged into Osmind, September 1, 2020; co-founders remained advisors
